# EU Alignment: Medpark on-premise Minutes of Meeting

Companion notes for `EU_Alignment_Scorecard_MoM.xlsx`. They are based on the prototype as it is on branch
`minutes-gemma3` (2026-09-26). Scores in the workbook are a **draft**, to agree with the mentor.

## Team answers (the 5 questions from the EU frameworks helper)

1. **Sector:** HealthTech. The solution is also cross-sector digital, since it covers executive and administrative meetings too.
2. **What it does:** it turns a hospital meeting recording (Romanian/Russian/English, with medical terms) into structured
   Minutes of Meeting (decisions, owners, deadlines). Everything runs on-premise with no internet.
3. **AI / personal data / device:** AI yes (Whisper ASR, pyannote diarisation, gemma3:4b LLM). Personal data yes: staff voices
   and patient health data spoken in the meeting. No physical device.
4. **Stage:** working prototype, tested on anonymised real Medpark recordings (11.7 min ICU handover: 19-21 of 25 reference
   facts; minutes ready ~45-90 s after the transcript on a 15 W laptop CPU). Not piloted with users yet. TRL about 4.
5. **Market / need:** pilot in Moldova (Medpark), then Romania as the first EU country. Next 12 months: testing in real
   conditions (a hospital pilot).

## A. Frameworks that likely apply

| Framework | Why it fits you | Scorecard criteria |
|---|---|---|
| GDPR Art. 9 + Art. 6 | Meetings discuss patients, so the transcript holds health data (a special category) | C1, C2 |
| GDPR Art. 25 + Art. 35 (DPIA) | Your main selling point is local processing. A DPIA is expected for health data + AI | C3, C4 |
| EU AI Act + ALTAI | Three AI models. Clinical meetings mean you must show human oversight and transparency | B2 |
| MDR | You need to decide early that the product is "meeting documentation" and not "clinical decision support" | D1, E4 |
| EHDS / digital health | The JSON minutes are a structured, interoperable record of hospital decisions | D1, D4 |
| NIS2 + Cyber Resilience Act | Hospitals are NIS2 entities; your software sits inside their network | B3, E4 |
| Digital Europe, EDIHs, TEFs | Local AI for public/health digitalisation; a health TEF or an EDIH is the place to test | B1, B4, E5 |
| Green Deal twin transition | A small CPU-only model is a real footprint argument (weak Green Deal fit otherwise) | A1, A3 |

## B. Funding and support that fits your stage (TRL ~4)

- **EDIH services** (test-before-invest, training): fits now; in-kind support to test in a hospital setting.
- **Horizon Europe** collaborative project with a hospital partner (Moldova is associated). EIC Pathfinder/Transition
  overlap at TRL 4; check eligibility with the mentor. EIC Accelerator is too early (TRL 6+).
- **Concrete 12-month step:** contact an EDIH from the official catalogue about a supported pilot at Medpark, and use the
  pilot results for a Horizon Europe or EIC application. Check current calls on the official EU portal; no deadlines are given here.

## C. Three questions to bring to your mentor

1. What written intended purpose keeps us out of MDR scope, and would a jury accept it as long as the minutes list clinical decisions?
2. What proof would Medpark's DPO and IT ask for before a pilot: a DPIA draft, a network test showing zero outbound traffic, access control?
3. Which accuracy measure convinces a medical board: our fact-recall benchmark, WER on code-switched speech, or clinician sign-off time?
